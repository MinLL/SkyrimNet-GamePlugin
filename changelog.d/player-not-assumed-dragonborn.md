---
title: NPCs call you the Dragonborn before you are one
area: Dialogue
kind: fix
---
About 50 character bios no longer refer to the player as the Dragonborn when it was only a stand-in for "the player", such as Leyathil's rescuer, Melana's companion, the Skaal freed from Miraak, or Cicero's Listener. Greybeards, dragons, Miraak and other characters whose story centers on the Dragonborn still speak of the Dragonborn.
