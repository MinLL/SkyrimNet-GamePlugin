---
title: Built-in presets rotate between models
area: LLM & Routing
kind: change
---
Applying Budget, Balanced or Premium from the dashboard now gives dialogue, combat and action selection a rotation of two or three models instead of one. Budget alternates the two Gemma-4 models. Balanced pairs Gemma-4 31B with DeepSeek v3.2 and adds DeepSeek v4.1 Flash to combat. Premium pairs Sonnet 4.6 with Gemma-4 31B. Balanced and Premium also lead the meta slot with DeepSeek v4.1 Flash. The Gemma slots run at temperature 1.0. Every model a preset uses now appears in the picker's Recommended list for that variant.
